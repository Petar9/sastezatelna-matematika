# GTM plan for the first ten paid families

Agreed 6 November 2026. Status: roadmap saved; the tasks below remain pending. This document does not record a paid launch or authorize outreach, purchases or deployment by itself.

## Goal and source documents

Release a complete paid experience for Bulgarian parents of fourth-graders preparing for maths competitions, acquire ten suitable new families, and evaluate learning usefulness, renewals and economics before expanding.

Build and verify the paid product before showing it to prospective buyers or starting outreach. Preserve the existing five-family pilot separately.

## Acquisition and operating rhythm

Prepare the approved introduction scripts and a private acquisition log before launch. The founder sends messages only after product readiness. Do not contact prospective buyers to validate the offer before that point.

Use both channels:

- Relevant personal introductions and useful contributions in parent communities where administrators permit them.
- Tutor relationships positioning the platform as practice between lessons, without promising tutoring or automatic marking of written proofs.

Record parent contact, channel/source, concrete preparation need, next step, buying decision and objection. Keep this log private and exclude children's school details, answers and learning history. Use the commercial guide's scripts, qualification questions and single appropriate follow-up.

Set outreach volume from available capacity and response quality rather than arbitrary weekly quotas. Compare qualified conversations, purchases and founder effort by channel; do not declare a winner from a few responses.

Keep sales, onboarding and support within three to five founder hours weekly. A starting allocation is approximately two hours for conversations/follow-up, one hour for onboarding/support and up to two hours for one useful resource or the main conversion obstacle. Reduce lower-priority activity or pause intake when support consumes capacity.

Choose additional articles from recurring parent questions and objections, supported by Search Console data when available. The two current candidates are:

- Preparation for Математика за всеки: competition-specific questions and suitable practice.
- From understanding a solution to solving a fresh problem: a useful example explaining the product's practice mechanism.

Neither candidate has a fixed first position. Do not invent search volumes or delay customer work to build a large blog.

## Success criteria and decision points

Retain the agreed commercial terms:

- **US$100/month per child**, charged upfront and monthly in USD; confirm final consumer-price disclosures before publication.
- **14-day first-payment cancellation/full-refund window**, without a minimum-use condition. This is not a free trial; later cancellation stops the next renewal under the commercial guide's terms.
- **Ten new paid families**, measured separately from the existing pilot; refunded families stay in the original cohort and refunds are reported separately.
- **Up to €100/month** for operating tools and acquisition, excluding development time and transaction fees. Keep currencies explicit and record costs rather than treating the ceiling as a spending target.

Use the commercial guide's definitions and provisional management gates:

| Signal | Initial review gate |
| --- | --- |
| Activation | At least 8/10 complete an adventure with a submitted whole-task answer within seven days of payment. |
| Continued practice | At least 7/10 have a completed whole-task attempt in three of their first four weeks; a homepage visit does not count. |
| First renewal | At least 7/10 complete the second monthly payment, with any grace-window recovery reported explicitly. |
| Learning usefulness | Review fresh independent solving separately from assisted answers, repeats and rewards; label insufficient evidence. |
| Economics and capacity | Record refunds, observed contribution, cash acquisition cost, sales effort and support time; stay within founder capacity. |

Review onboarding and support weekly, using each family's payment-relative observation window and Europe/Sofia for displayed dates. Report counts and denominators rather than percentages without context. Do not wait for ten families to fix an obvious access or usability problem; assess full-cohort targets only when the relevant windows have matured.

If results fall short, distinguish fit, clarity, level mismatch, payment friction, price objections, content exhaustion and seasonality. Keep the US$100 price stable for the initial intake and change one major offer/channel variable at a time for later tests.

Inspect first and second renewals before materially increasing acquisition. Expansion requires useful learning, repeat purchases, manageable support and sustainable contribution; ten customers alone do not establish PMF.

## Boundaries and maintenance

Ambassador programs, commissions, referral discounts, paid ads, extra pricing tiers and additional grades remain out of scope. Tutor relationships in this plan do not include an affiliate arrangement.

Keep task status current as evidence becomes available and record actual checks and release status in the project task log. Do not mark a task complete because its specification exists. This roadmap introduces no application code, public API, schema, learner-data change or deployment.
