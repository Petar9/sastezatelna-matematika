# Competition content workflow

Prepare grade 4 Олимпиада first, then НВО. Both remain unpublished until the supplied sources and the full collection have been reviewed. Do not invent missing competition questions, answers, figures, sources, or transcripts. The owner supplies PDFs, solutions, and tutor video links; request missing material and keep the collection in draft until it arrives.

## Intake and provenance

1. Archive each supplied PDF, solution, and video link privately under `materials/`. Keep originals unchanged and out of Git and deployment uploads. Record the source title, year, round, grade, page or timestamp, source file or link, and date received in a private source inventory. Note which answer key and solution correspond to each problem. Do not place private source files or learner history in public assets.
2. Archive each available video's spoken transcript as Markdown alongside its source. Record the video link and any uncertainty or missing audio; correct the transcript against the source rather than filling gaps from memory. If a transcript or video is unavailable, mark it missing.
3. Extract the original wording, choices and figures from the supplied sources. Preserve the problem number and provenance. Compare the extraction with the source and check each answer against the supplied key and solution; flag discrepancies for human resolution. Do not silently repair ambiguous or illegible material.

## Prepare learning content

For each verified problem, map the relevant grade 4 skills and prepare a worked solution with exactly three meaningful steps. Check that each step follows from the preceding one and that the final answer agrees with the source. Add fresh practice problems and harder variants for the same skills; write and independently check their answers and explanations. Distinguish original source problems from authored variants and retain the original provenance. Validate diagrams, units, choices, and Bulgarian wording against the intended problem.

Keep the collection in draft during preparation. Record each problem's source reference, skill mapping, answer and review status, including unresolved discrepancies. Mark missing sources and questions explicitly; do not publish partial or unreviewed material as a complete collection.

## Review and publication gate

A human reviewer checks every original against its source, every answer and three-step explanation, each fresh practice problem and harder variant, figures and skill mappings. Resolve discrepancies and record the review before marking a problem ready. Only enable a competition after the complete collection passes review and the app's practice, sessions, maps, evidence and awards are tested within that competition. Check switching between published competitions preserves unfinished work and never mixes progress.

Publish Олимпиада only after its gate passes; then follow the same process for НВО. Use the normal development and release workflow, preserving existing course history and backups. A local draft or passing local tests is not a published or independently verified live release.
