# Educational product decisions

Adopted educational decisions for Математически открития. Update these bullets when an educational policy changes.

- **A short adventure is the main teaching entry.** “Започни приключение” starts five problems; “Продължи приключението” resumes saved work. Five visible stepping stones give children a clear finish.
- **Use a balanced mixture:** target two familiar/review problems, two addressing current difficulties, and one new or harder problem. Familiar success builds confidence, recovery addresses gaps, and stretch supports advancement. Adapt when evidence or fresh material is unavailable; do not force harder work before prerequisites are ready.
- **Interleave topics and methods.** Choose from the learning map and saved evidence rather than requiring children to choose their weakest topic. Topic choice remains a secondary, always available option.
- **Diagnosis is brief and adaptive.** Use original competition problems, at most three within the five-problem quest. Diagnose on first use and later only for unclear difficulties after teaching/recovery; reuse existing evidence instead of testing every visit.
- **Competition diagnosis is routing evidence.** A few answers cannot establish overall ability, readiness for a competition, or permanent mastery. Apply ordinary confirmation rules to diagnostic answers.
- **Solve the current problem together in three parts.** “Решаваме заедно” starts a concrete calculation with its reason; “Следваща стъпка” continues the solution and finally shows the answer and check. Keep previous parts visible, use the exact question’s numbers, and allow an answer after any part. Requesting any part makes the answer helped, never independent.
- **Practise immediately after difficulty.** Show the worked solution after a wrong or revealed answer, then prioritise a fresh problem for that skill within the five-problem adventure. End diagnosis early when help or difficulty identifies a need. Do not insert a second generic lesson; help consumes no problem slot.
- **Require fresh independent evidence.** An initially successful objective needs two independent successes. Hinted success needs one fresh independent confirmation unless a prior wrong/revealed gap already requires two. Wrong or revealed answers need two consecutive fresh independent successes; another error resets recovery.
- **Keep evidence honest.** Distinguish independent, hinted, incorrect and revealed attempts. Grade selected/numeric answers; optional written reasoning is saved for adults and is not automatically assessed as proof.
- **Separate teaching concepts from visible skill leaves.** Keep reviewed concepts and prerequisites for adaptive teaching. Across all 17 islands, show one leaf per course skill, using only that skill’s evidence; the five Рачешки задачи skills therefore have five leaves.
- **Use reviewed prerequisite relationships.** The map guides comfortable practice, gap clarification and appropriate challenges. A multi-concept failure does not mark every associated concept as a demonstrated gap. All islands remain selectable.
- **Separate completion from achievement.** Completing all five problems earns a quest stamp and opens the owl chest, including when help was needed. The progress message must describe actual session evidence.
- **Retain earned academic owl awards.** Doctor requires all topic objectives confirmed. Professor requires two further consecutive independent harder successes across different objectives when available. These are course awards, not claims of permanent expertise.
- **Review remains optional.** Offer a fresh retention check after two days without blocking another topic, question, award or completion.
- **Protect motivation and autonomy.** No timers, lost lives, daily-streak penalties or reward removal for mistakes. Children can pause, change topics or stop after a quest; animation respects reduced-motion preferences.
- **Preserve continuity.** Save exact questions, help levels, lessons and recovery across reloads and mode changes. Keep quest work separate from topic practice and preserve existing progress during upgrades.
- **Use reviewed Bulgarian material and deterministic teaching.** Keep PDF/video provenance, distinguish originals from authored variants, and avoid live model-generated grading or teaching in this flow.
- **Defer standalone migration.** Complete and verify the kids experience first; extracting it into its own app is a subsequent project.

- **Keep the next action visible.** Use one persistent bottom button for checking, continuing or opening the treasure; reserve its space and follow the mobile keyboard. Keep hints and topic choice secondary, maintain readable diagrams and at least 44px touch targets, and never advance automatically.
- **Use compact, friendly Bulgarian.** Call the journey “приключение” and the backward-solving topic “Рачешки задачи”; retain “обратни действия” when explaining the method. Say “Верен отговор” to the child, celebrate with a small emoji, and retain the independent/helped distinction in saved evidence and clear help labels.
- **Separate earned recognition from current readiness.** Preserve Doctor/Professor decorations when a competence needs practice or recall. The leaf state reflects current course evidence, not permanent mastery; never infer award levels for individual competences from a topic award.
- **Make early effort visible.** A completed attempt or reviewed solution opens a competence bud; the first correct answer, including one with a hint, adds a sparkle. Keep these small discoveries visible inside topic islands and distinguish helped answers in details. Opening a topic or requesting a hint alone earns no growth.
- **Celebrate the next small discovery immediately.** Show one compact growth message after an answer, prioritising confirmed competence over first correctness over first effort; Doctor/Professor celebrations take priority. Restore historical growth quietly, derive milestones only from primary-objective evidence, and retain early discoveries when later practice is needed.

- **Show “Моите малки открития” instead of adult evidence panels.** Use three earned milestones: “Първа стъпка” for any completed attempt or reviewed solution, “Първо откритие” for the first correct answer (including a labelled hinted answer), and “Вече го умея” for course confirmation. Opening a topic or requesting a hint alone does not count. These are discovery milestones, not a percentage of mastery or a new points currency.
- **Keep discoveries while showing the next learning goal.** Earned milestones remain visible after later difficulty. Show the current independent-answer requirement from the saved streak and target, with “Да затвърдим” during recovery and “Да си припомним” when review is due. Never change confirmation or owl-award rules to make the display advance.
- **Use exploration language with precise results.** Say “Да открием”, “Откриваме заедно”, “Първо откритие” and “Да си припомним” around the map. Keep “Верен отговор”, “С помощ”, mathematical instructions and skill titles clear; use discovery rather than victory language in celebrations and accessibility labels.


- **Explore islands without a prescribed order.** Replace the tree and climbing owl with one responsive explorer’s map. All 17 islands stay available; the owl accompanies the selected island rather than implying a bottom-to-top curriculum. Remove the global discovery counter.
- **Show one discovery bar per island.** “Моите малки открития · X/Y” counts the three retained milestones per skill, with blue earned progress on a muted horizontal track. Five skills mean 15 discoveries. Show milestones and the next learning goal only when a skill is opened; no repeated progress cards.
- **Keep session completion separate.** The five-stop adventure path shows the current session; island discoveries accumulate across sessions and never reset daily. These counts are not a mastery percentage.
- **Keep recognition and practice inside each island.** Show compact skill rows, Doctor/Professor awards and one “Да упражним” action in the selected island. Preserve earned awards and announce new growth once per visit, respecting reduced motion.
- **Use one home control.** “← Начало” returns to the start screen without discarding the saved question, help or progress.

- **Make the world a place to explore.** The default “Карта” is a pan-and-zoom sea with 17 illustrated islands at fixed topic-ID positions. No route, lock or island number dictates learning order. “Теми” keeps the compact accessible topic grid; both open the same skills and exercises. The wheel scrolls the page; touch dragging, pinch, zoom buttons and “Цялата карта” control the map.
- **Let islands grow from real discoveries.** Each skill contributes a sprout after a completed attempt, flowers after its first correct answer (including helped answers), and a mature plant after confirmation. Decorations accumulate and remain after later difficulty. A Doctor flag and a golden Professor landmark celebrate existing topic awards without altering their rules. Restore historical scenery quietly and briefly highlight new growth on return; respect reduced motion.
- **Let the owl accompany exploration.** The owl joins the selected island’s heading, never waits for a correct answer to move, and never implies a mandatory climb. Keep exact blue discovery counts in island details and the topic grid, with individual milestones available on demand.
- **Collect souvenirs, not a new currency.** “Моите съкровища” illustrates the existing saved adventure stamps and keeps their actual achievement text. Five completed problems still open the chest, including when help was needed. Map cameras are temporary; saved answers, help, evidence and reward storage are unchanged.

- Математически открития is an independent application; educational content and grading run without an AI provider.
- Local progress is private to this computer; hosted progress belongs to a parent-owned child profile and is never combined across children.
- Changing the selected child in another tab cannot retarget a question already open in this tab.

- Keep competition journeys separate. Each child has independent practice, discoveries, confirmations and awards for each competition; switching never discards unfinished work. Publish reviewed collections only, starting with Олимпиада before НВО, all for grade 4.
- Keep the pilot private and small. Parents use approved Google accounts; children use nicknames. At most five child profiles are active across all families. An invitation request joins a private, manually reviewed list and grants no access.
