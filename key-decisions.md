# Educational product decisions

Adopted educational decisions for Математически открития. Update these bullets when an educational policy changes.

- **A short adventure is the main teaching entry.** “Започни приключение” starts five problems; “Продължи приключението” resumes saved work. One horizontal five-task bar gives children a clear finish.
- **Pair, then interleave methods.** Open a returning adventure with an unseen application of a previously successful method. Follow success or difficulty with one fresh related task, then change methods. The five-task adventure has no mandatory harder fifth question; use existing prerequisites and evidence rather than a fixed 2/2/1 quota.
- **Interleave topics and methods.** Choose from the learning map and saved evidence rather than requiring children to choose their weakest topic. Topic choice remains a secondary, always available option.
- **Diagnosis is brief and adaptive.** Use original competition problems, at most three within the five-problem quest. Diagnose on first use and later only for unclear difficulties after teaching/recovery; reuse existing evidence instead of testing every visit.
- **Competition diagnosis is routing evidence.** A few answers cannot establish overall ability, readiness for a competition, or permanent mastery. Apply ordinary confirmation rules to diagnostic answers.
- **Solve the current problem together in three parts.** “Подсказка” starts a concrete calculation with its reason; “Следваща подсказка” continues the solution and finally shows the answer and check. Keep previous parts visible, use the exact question’s numbers, and allow an answer after any part. Requesting any part makes the answer helped, never independent.
- **Practise immediately after difficulty.** Show the worked solution after a wrong or revealed answer, then prioritise one fresh problem for the same method within the five-problem adventure; after that pair, switch methods even if the child is still struggling. End diagnosis early when help or difficulty identifies a need. Do not insert a second generic lesson; help consumes no problem slot.
- **Require fresh independent evidence.** An initially successful objective needs two independent successes. Hinted success needs one fresh independent confirmation unless a prior wrong/revealed gap already requires two. Wrong or revealed answers need two consecutive fresh independent successes; another error resets recovery.
- **Keep evidence honest.** Distinguish independent, hinted, incorrect and revealed attempts. Grade selected/numeric answers. The optional written-reasoning box is removed from the child UI; retain historical reasoning without automatically assessing it as proof.
- **Separate teaching concepts from visible skill markers.** Keep reviewed concepts and prerequisites for adaptive teaching. Across all 17 islands, show one marker per course skill using only that skill’s evidence; Рачешки задачи therefore has five markers.
- **Use reviewed prerequisite relationships.** The map guides comfortable practice, gap clarification and appropriate challenges. A multi-concept failure does not mark every associated concept as a demonstrated gap. All islands remain selectable.
- **Separate completion from achievement.** Completing all five problems earns a quest stamp and opens the treasure chest, including when help was needed. The progress message must describe actual session evidence.
- **Retain earned academic awards.** Doctor requires all topic objectives confirmed. Professor requires two further consecutive independent harder successes across different objectives when available. These are course awards, not claims of permanent expertise.
- **Review remains optional.** Offer a fresh retention check after two days without blocking another topic, question, award or completion.
- **Protect motivation and autonomy.** No timers, lost lives, daily-streak penalties or reward removal for mistakes. Children can pause, change topics or stop after a quest; animation respects reduced-motion preferences.
- **Preserve continuity.** Save exact questions, help levels, lessons and recovery across reloads and mode changes. Keep quest work separate from topic practice and preserve existing progress during upgrades.
- **Use reviewed Bulgarian material and deterministic teaching.** Keep PDF/video provenance, distinguish originals from authored variants, and avoid live model-generated grading or teaching in this flow.
- **Defer standalone migration.** Complete and verify the kids experience first; extracting it into its own app is a subsequent project.

- **Keep the next action visible.** Use one persistent bottom button for checking, continuing or opening the treasure; reserve its space and follow the mobile keyboard. Keep hints and topic choice secondary, maintain readable diagrams and at least 44px touch targets, and never advance automatically.
- **Use compact, friendly Bulgarian.** Call the journey “приключение” and the backward-solving topic “Рачешки задачи”; retain “обратни действия” when explaining the method. Say “Верен отговор” to the child, celebrate with a small emoji, and retain the independent/helped distinction in saved evidence and clear help labels.
- **Separate earned recognition from current readiness.** Preserve Doctor/Professor decorations when a competence needs practice or recall. Markers show retained discoveries, not permanent mastery; never infer award levels for individual competences from a topic award.
- **Make early effort visible.** A completed attempt or reviewed solution earns a blue flag; the first correct answer, including one with help, earns a teal sparkle; confirmation earns a gold checked medallion. One marker changes state per skill. Opening a topic or requesting help alone earns nothing.
- **Celebrate combined discoveries immediately.** Show one compact gold gain per answer, combining simultaneous milestones with an optional breakdown. Use a brief coin flight to the wallet and respect reduced motion. Keep Doctor/Professor recognition separate; restore historical discoveries quietly and retain them after later difficulty.

- **Show learning progress during the exercise.** Remove the repeated “Моите малки открития” title and expanded island evidence cards. Use three earned milestones: “Първа стъпка” for any completed attempt or reviewed solution, “Първо откритие” for the first correct answer (including a labelled hinted answer), and “Вече го умея” for course confirmation. Opening a topic or requesting a hint alone does not count. These discovery counts are separate from gold and are not a percentage of mastery.
- **Keep discoveries while showing the next learning goal.** Earned milestones remain visible after later difficulty. Show the current independent-answer requirement from the saved streak and target, with “Да затвърдим” during recovery and “Да си припомним” when review is due. Never change confirmation or award rules to make the display advance.
- **Use exploration language with precise results.** Say “Да открием”, “Откриваме заедно”, “Първо откритие” and “Да си припомним” around the map. Keep “Верен отговор”, “С помощ”, mathematical instructions and skill titles clear; use discovery rather than victory language in celebrations and accessibility labels.


- **Explore islands without a prescribed order.** Replace the tree and climbing mascot with one responsive explorer’s map. All 17 islands stay available; the otter accompanies the selected island rather than implying a bottom-to-top curriculum. Remove the global discovery counter.
- **Show collected gold per island.** Use a right-aligned coin beside X/Y and a gold horizontal bar, without a repeated visible label in the topic grid and island details. Read retained awards from the same ledger as the wallet; The maximum combines 35 per skill with +5 for each distinct prepared problem entitlement; full originals and their parts count once. Gold measures the collection, not mastery. Show individual learning milestones and the next goal during practice and answer feedback; island rows retain only their short status and marker.
- **Keep session completion separate.** The five-stop adventure path shows the current session; island discoveries accumulate across sessions and never reset daily. These counts are not a mastery percentage.
- **Keep recognition and practice inside each island.** Show compact skill rows, Doctor/Professor awards and one “Да упражним” action in the selected island. Preserve earned awards and announce new growth once per visit, respecting reduced motion.
- **Use one home control.** “← Начало” returns to the start screen without discarding the saved question, help or progress.

- **Make the world a place to explore.** The default “Карта” is a pan-and-zoom sea with 17 illustrated islands at fixed topic-ID positions. No route, lock or island number dictates learning order. “Теми” keeps the compact accessible topic grid; both open the same skills and exercises. The wheel scrolls the page; touch dragging, pinch, zoom buttons and “Цялата карта” control the map.
- **Mark real discoveries on islands.** Replace the former tree leaves and plants with one blue flag, teal sparkle or gold checked medallion per skill. Retain the highest earned state after later difficulty. A Doctor flag and golden Professor landmark celebrate existing topic awards without changing their rules; historical markers restore quietly.
- **Let the otter accompany exploration.** The otter joins the selected island’s heading, never waits for a correct answer to move, and never implies a mandatory climb. Keep exact collected gold in island details and the topic grid, with individual learning milestones shown during practice.
- **Collect sea souvenirs alongside gold.** “Моите съкровища” illustrates existing saved adventure stamps and keeps their actual achievement text. Preview the next souvenir, reveal it after five completed problems including helped/revealed work, then show the latest on home. Four original illustrations repeat: starfish, pearl shell, compass and ship in a bottle; do not imply an unlimited set of unique items. Completion adds no bonus gold.

- Математически открития is an independent application; educational content and grading run without an AI provider.
- Local progress is private to this computer; hosted progress belongs to a parent-owned child profile and is never combined across children.
- Changing the selected child in another tab cannot retarget a question already open in this tab.

- Keep competition journeys separate. Each child has independent practice, discoveries, confirmations and awards for each competition; switching never discards unfinished work. Publish reviewed collections only, starting with Олимпиада before НВО, all for grade 4.
- Keep the pilot private and small. Parents use approved Google accounts; children use nicknames. At most five child profiles are active across all families. An invitation request joins a private, manually reviewed list and grants no access.

- **Welcome children with an otter explorer.** Use the same original mascot across setup, home, map, feedback and earned awards. Warm cream reading surfaces, pale blue sea, teal actions and blue progress establish a distinct identity. Keep source diagrams unaltered on white bordered panels.
- **Recognise small steps where they happen.** Show the active skill’s compact discovery bar during topic practice and directly with answer feedback. A completed attempt adds the first step, helped or independent correctness adds the first discovery, and existing course confirmation completes the bar. Requesting help alone adds nothing; preserve helped labels and the next independent-answer goal.
- **Show earned awards rather than empty placeholders.** Doctor and Professor appear only once achieved. Remove repeated award requirements from island, challenge, reward and completion views; retain the underlying rules and celebrations. Keep earned recognition after later difficulty and restore it without replaying a celebration.
- **Keep one clear next action.** Home prioritises starting or resuming the five-task adventure, with topics secondary. Replace the repeated stage strip with one compact adventure-completion bar; its progress counts completed problems, including helped/revealed work, separately from skill confirmation.

- **Explain with concrete actions for grade 4.** Prefer “намери колко най-много” and “подреди от най-голямата към най-малката” to academic shorthand. Keep mathematical meaning, original competition statements and answer keys intact; show revised help for identical saved questions without rewriting their learning history.

- **Retain skill gold and reward fresh problems.** Award 5 for the first completed attempt or revealed solution, 10 for first correctness (helped included), and 20 for independent confirmation, at most 35 per skill. Add +5 once for a fresh correctly completed problem, helped included. Full originals and all their parts share one bonus. Combine simultaneous gains. Help alone, reveal alone, repeated answers, reconfirmation and adventure completion add no problem bonus. Gold is collectible only, with no shop, deductions or penalties.
- **Keep one wallet per child.** Sum gold across that child's competition ledgers while keeping learning, confirmations and academic awards separate by competition. Never combine children or families. Validate awards on the server and save them with revision-guarded learning updates, not a mutable shared balance.
- **Credit existing history quietly.** Project eligible historical milestones without changing answers or resetting progress; persist the ledger with the next accepted command. GET/reload never replays old rewards. Preserve earned gold after mistakes.
- **Make the current adventure the home focus.** Use one start/resume button, a horizontal five-stop blue route and moving boat, saved completion, next-treasure preview and latest souvenir. Topic choice and manual-topic resume are secondary. The boat advances for completed work, including help, separately from skill confirmation.
- **Keep the map readable and optional.** Open both competitions at the whole-map fit; only zoom into an island after the child selects it. Retain every island and the accessible topic grid, and keep pan gestures from swallowing island-button activation. Learning order stays adaptive rather than dictated by geography.

- **Separate the website from its competitions.** Use “Състезателна математика” as the website identity on the header, landing, onboarding, About page and browser title. On home use the competition dropdown without a repeated subtitle; during practice show the active competition title and grade from the catalogue; keep “За сайта” concise. Retain dark teal primary actions, blue learning progress and gold rewards.


## Олимпиада - draft content decisions (2026-11-03)

- **Four islands for this collection.** The approved municipal rounds 2022–2025 share Числови загадки, Геометрични открития, Истории с числа and Логически загадки, with 4/3/3/2 skills. The existing 17-island collection keeps its map and history.
- **Finite practice with concrete help.** Each of the 12 skills has three fresh core exercises and two harder variants for confirmation and extension; eligible real competition tasks come first. Every problem has three concrete partial-solution parts. Existing five-task adaptive selection, independent confirmation and gold rules remain the same.
- **Keep uncertain sources visible to reviewers.** Archive the ambiguous 2023 problem 3, but exclude it from automatic grading. Prepare 2024 only as a draft using the supplied tutor explanation and independent calculations; the official solution PDF is missing. A human must review the entire collection before publishing it.
- **Preserve originals and label additions.** Keep original statements and cropped diagrams, with clearly labelled learning prompts. Roman-numeral multiple-choice options are authored teaching aids, not original exam options. Full automatic transcripts remain private reference material; extracted mathematical answers are checked separately.

- **Choose competitions from one compact dropdown.** Show the current competition, mark its option, and display the owner-approved “Трудно” badge for both current competitions. This is a competition label, not a change to adaptive difficulty or rewards.

- **Start a chosen skill directly.** Each compact island skill row opens practice restricted to that skill; the island’s “Да упражним” button remains adaptive across the island. Keep separate unfinished questions, hint levels, lessons and feedback for each chosen skill, whole-island practice and the adventure. Do not fall back to another skill when its prepared tasks are exhausted; offer “Към острова”. Skill selection changes routing only, never confirmation, gold or Doctor/Professor rules.


## Real competition tasks first - approved 2026-11-03

- **Begin with the archive in both competitions.** Adventures, diagnostics, islands and exact subtopics prefer an unseen, gradable real task within the selected learning goal. Show its actual competition, year, round and task number; preserve the original wording and diagrams. Keep a read-only link to the original statement without publishing private PDFs or answer packs.
- **Use similar exercises to confirm understanding.** Prefer another eligible real question for that skill; when none remains, use reviewed authored practice explicitly marked „Учебно упражнение“, without claiming it was given in a competition. In adventures, recovery pairs one fresh application of the same method before switching; exact-subtopic practice remains restricted to the selected skill. Keep five tasks and the maximum of three diagnostic questions.
- **Work through originals in order.** New multi-answer originals show one answer field at a time and preserve intermediate progress. Count the whole original once for history and adventure completion; help or an error in an earlier answer prevents a later answer from being classified as a wholly independent solution. Existing pending all-field questions retain their saved contract.
- **Three concrete partial solutions.** „Подсказка“ and „Следваща подсказка“ reveal successive worked steps, with calculations and reasoning. Assisted work remains distinct from independent confirmation.
- **Check retention after two days.** Schedule review for each confirmed skill, even before its whole island is completed. Review does not block advancement or earned rewards, and repeated or overlapping originals/teaching parts cannot become new independent evidence or pay gold again.
- **Preserve learning history.** Select by task identity, past exposure and pending work rather than saved catalogue position. Keep saved answers, hints, unfinished adventures, gold and Doctor/Professor awards; no progress reset or content-version migration is needed.
- **Do not grade uncertain sources.** Olympiad 2023 problem 3 remains excluded as underdetermined; its overlapping-groups skill therefore currently uses clearly marked authored exercises. Olympiad is still available locally only until source caveats receive publication approval.


## Compact source labels and untouched-task replacement - approved 2026-11-03

- **Keep task footers short.** Display only the competition title and actual year for real competition tasks, linked to the original statement. Keep full provenance internally. Authored confirmation exercises have no competition year; display the competition title alone rather than inventing one.
- **Refresh untouched legacy exercises.** Replace saved authored questions with an eligible unused real task for the same skill and practice mode. Preserve anything already answered, hinted, revealed or started as a lesson or subcondition. Keep adventure position, history, skill evidence, awards and gold; use normal revision-guarded persistence, without rewriting learner files on GET.

## Method-based practice and problem gold - approved 2026-11-04

- **Use editorial entry points, not guessed difficulty.** Each existing method has a documented provisional real introductory task where a gradable source exists. Do not infer difficulty from question number, text length or answer format. Treat these choices as pilot hypotheses, not calibrated levels.
- **Prefer real applications across years.** For the related follow-up, another unseen real problem in the same method takes precedence over authored practice for the exact previous skill. Keep source overlap and pending-question exclusions; authored exercises fill finite source gaps. No new islands or skill identifiers.
- **Recover missed problem gold through future success.** GET and historical projection never backfill +5. A future correctly completed attempt, helped included, can earn an unpaid entitlement once; originals and their parts share it. Preserve existing skill gold and wallet balances; show one combined gain notice.
- **Keep a reachable island treasure target.** On first persisted introduction, freeze each child’s island target from unique problem entitlements plus skill milestones. Additional content earns wallet gold without moving that target or closing its chest. The single gold bar and chest show collection progress; Doctor/Professor still depend on the academic rules.
- **Test before expanding.** Keep the existing 17/4 islands, confirmation and Doctor/Professor rules, saved work and five-task flow. Observe the maximum five-child pilot before adding measured difficulty models, new rewards or more categories.


## Reachable treasure and reliable continuation - approved 2026-11-04

- **Keep gold goals visible.** Show one horizontal island gold bar, a coin beside the count and a small chest that opens at the saved target. Do not add another skill-completion bar. More content can increase the wallet beyond the fixed target.
- **Keep learning available after awards.** Remaining hard problems stay accessible after Professor when prerequisites are ready; retain real-source priority, exposure exclusions and confirmation rules. A due retention review is explicitly revision, not a new independent problem bonus.
- **Finish finite adventures honestly.** If only one to four eligible tasks remain, finish at that actual count and grant one souvenir, without completion gold. With no eligible work, show a terminal state and the next review date when available; never start an empty loop.
- **Save work without submitting it.** Numeric and choice drafts belong to the child, competition, task and current subcondition. Save them through the revision-guarded course store; restore across Home, Pause and reload. Reject conflicts with a newer device instead of overwriting or submitting stale answers. Drafts and requesting hints alone add no evidence or rewards.
- **Keep feedback concise and consistent.** Use neutral continuation copy, a revisit goal instead of a contradictory mastered label, and grouped souvenir types with counts. Reward explanations read shared amounts rather than duplicated numbers.

## 2026-11-04 - Reviewed hard-problem multiplier

- New distinct correct problems pay 10 gold; editorially hard problems pay 15 (×1.5). The same rate applies with hints. Reveal alone does not earn problem gold. This supersedes the prospective +5 rate only for future awards.
- Difficulty belongs to the actual reasoning in each problem, not the competition badge, order, text length, answer format, number size or existing hard-selection pool. Combined methods, dependent reasoning, cases and proof/optimization can justify hard; record a rationale in content/problem-difficulty.json. Rate authored practice separately; source parts inherit the original.
- One source original, its parts and scaffolds share one entitlement. Replays/reloads cannot farm gold. An old award without amount still pays exactly 5; new records persist the paid amount. Existing history and per-child island targets remain unchanged; new targets sum weighted unique problem rewards plus unchanged 5/10/20 skill milestones.
- Owner chose reward-only differentiation: do not increase hard-task frequency or change five-task adventures, adaptive selection, confirmation or Doctor/Professor criteria for this multiplier. Preview only the available problem reward, with coin and 10 or 15 · ×1,5.
- Ratings are provisional editorial judgements to test with the pilot children, not measured difficulty or publication approval. See docs/problem-difficulty-review.md and docs/content-workflow.md.


## 2026-11-04 - Focused exploration and reachable help

- Selecting an island opens its compact skill view; the adventure dashboard and whole map return when leaving that island. Keep the selected island and map/list choice available when returning from practice.
- Navigation to Home or Map is a client view change, not a learning action. Preserve task, draft answer, hints and outcome; map links must land at the map.
- Show one compact adventure route/count and keep Подсказка beside Провери отговора. Feedback uses one outcome, combined earned gold and one next learning goal; assisted work remains clearly labelled.
- The whole-map phone view keeps all islands accessible and permits normal vertical page scrolling. Zoomed-in maps allow dragging; earned discoveries and awards remain visible without untried decorative circles crowding the overview. Educational and reward criteria are unchanged.


## 2026-11-04 - Optional child Google access

- The parent creates a nickname profile first. Adding the child's Google email is optional during onboarding, with “Не сега”, and remains available in Profiles.
- Child login reuses the existing child ID and saved competition history. It never creates another learner or sends an invitation email. Parents retain family management; children can access only their own learner profile.
- Link only a confirmed Google identity through a private server-only mapping. One child per email and one Google identity per child; changing or removing the email revokes the old access. Inactive children and families without parent approval cannot sign in.
- Preserve course JSON, revisions, earned gold, awards and the five-active-child limit. Apply the additive login migration before deploying dependent code.
