# Current product and educational decisions

Current approved rules for Състезателна математика, consolidated 2026-11-06. This document governs product behavior; [the design system](docs/design-system.md) governs presentation. Update the relevant section when an approved policy changes. Dated approvals and superseded decisions remain in the [decision history](docs/archive/decision-history.md); completed implementation and release evidence remain in the [implementation history](docs/archive/implementation-history.md).

## Learning and selection

- The main entry starts or resumes a five-step adventure. Pair a successful familiar method with a fresh related application, then interleave methods. There is no mandatory harder fifth question or fixed 2/2/1 quota. Topic and exact-skill choice remain available.
- Prefer eligible unseen gradable originals within the learning goal, ideally across years. Related real applications of the method take precedence over authored practice for the exact previous skill. Use reviewed, clearly labelled authored exercises for confirmation or documented source gaps.
- Diagnosis uses at most three original problems within the adventure, on first use or for unclear difficulty after teaching/recovery. Reuse evidence; diagnosis is routing evidence, not a measure of overall ability or competition readiness.
- After an incorrect or revealed answer, show the worked solution and prioritize one fresh related problem before changing methods. End diagnosis early when difficulty identifies a need. Help adds no problem slot or second generic lesson.
- Use reviewed concepts and prerequisites; a multi-concept failure does not prove a gap in every concept. All islands stay selectable; map geography never dictates order.
- Select by source identity, exposure and saved work, not catalogue position. Exact-skill practice never silently switches skills when exhausted. Finish finite adventures at their actual one-to-four task count; with no eligible work show the return/review action, never an empty loop.

## Help, evidence and confirmation

- Reveal three concrete worked-solution parts using the current problem's numbers, calculation and reasoning. Keep earlier parts visible and allow an answer after each. Any hint makes correctness assisted, never independent.
- Keep independent, hinted, incorrect and revealed outcomes distinct. Grade numeric/selected answers deterministically; retain historical written reasoning without treating it as automatically assessed proof. No live AI teaching or grading in this flow.
- An initially successful objective needs two fresh independent successes. Hinted success needs one fresh independent confirmation unless an earlier wrong/revealed gap requires two. Wrong or revealed work needs two consecutive fresh independent successes; another error resets recovery.
- Work through new multi-answer originals one field at a time. Count the whole original once for adventure completion and fresh evidence; earlier help or error prevents wholly independent classification. Preserve the contract of pending legacy all-field tasks.
- Repeated originals, overlapping parts and scaffolds cannot become fresh independent evidence. Offer optional retention review after two days for each confirmed skill, without blocking advancement, other topics or earned awards.
- Preserve exact pending questions, drafts, hint levels, subcondition position and recovery across reloads/navigation. Keep adventure, island and chosen-skill work separate. Replace untouched legacy authored questions only with compatible unused real tasks; preserve started work. The historical owner-authorized replacement of started Olympiad test work was a one-time exception, not ongoing permission.

## Recognition and motivation

- Show one earned marker per skill: completed attempt/reviewed solution, first correct answer (assisted included), then confirmation. Merely opening a topic or requesting help earns no milestone. Retain the highest earned state after later difficulty; show the current recall/recovery goal honestly.
- Doctor requires every topic objective confirmed. Professor requires two further consecutive independent harder successes across different objectives when available. Preserve earned awards after later difficulty; remaining eligible hard practice stays accessible after Professor. These awards are not claims of permanent mastery.
- Adventure completion earns one stamp/souvenir, including helped/revealed work, without bonus gold. Describe actual session evidence; the four souvenir illustrations repeat and are not an unlimited unique collection.
- No timers, lost lives, streak penalties, reward deductions or forced advancement. Children can pause, change topics or stop. Keep learning milestones, adventure completion and collectible gold distinct.

## Competitions and source integrity

- Keep competition → island → skill → several problems. Competitions have separate practice, discoveries, confirmations, awards and unfinished sessions. Preserve stable IDs/content compatibility when adding years; enrich existing islands before adding categories.
- Published pilot collections (before the local connected-problem revision): Математика за всеки (17 islands, 2023–2026), Олимпиада (four islands, 2022–2025), НВО (10 islands/27 skills, 100 originals from 2022–2025). Catalogue badges are Трудно / Трудно / Лесно; they do not set per-problem reward difficulty. Count full originals separately from subconditions and authored exercises.
- The owner approved publication of all three on 2026-11-04 despite disclosed outstanding final human review and the missing 2024 Olympiad official solutions. This is a specific pilot exception, not evidence that review is complete or approval for future unreviewed content. Follow the [competition checklist](docs/content-workflow.md).
- Olympiad 2023 problem 3 stays excluded from automatic grading as underdetermined. The 2023 Математика за всеки M1-06/M1-17 clarification decisions remain pending; do not enable them without resolution. Keep finite-source gaps honest instead of counting repetitions as new knowledge.
- Preserve original wording, answer choices and diagrams. Label clarifications and authored teaching aids separately. Check mathematics against official answers where available; record missing/conflicting sources. Tutor explanations inform methods, not official-key status. Full PDFs, transcripts and answer packs stay private.
- Footer format: originals “[competition] · [year]”; practice “По [competition] · [source year]”. Resolve actual provenance; never invent an exercise year or expose internal task codes. Use consistent accessible links only to allowed original statements. Approved 2026-11-05.

## Connected originals - approved local revision 2026-11-06

- Show shared context and the current natural subpart with all its answer fields together. Keep the full original and previously completed subparts in collapsed disclosures; do not leak future answers. Unlettered originals remain one unit, regardless of scaffold count.
- Keep each whole original in one island: Математика за всеки 16 (geometry split into „Обиколки на фигури“ and „Лица и мрежи“), Олимпиада 4, НВО 6, Пролетни 4. Navigation groups project existing skills without rewriting historical topic IDs or earned rewards. Preserve blocked sources.
- Adventures count five natural steps and can finish during an original; the next adventure resumes its remaining subparts. Preserve exact drafts, help and position across navigation and reload. Previously assigned tasks retain their historical grading contract.
- Reset hints for each natural subpart; earlier dependent help still affects independence. Sibling subparts of one original cannot alone confirm a skill. Third-hint exclusion applies to that subpart including milestones.

## Profiles, access and saved work

- This is a standalone application. Local history stays private to the computer; hosted history belongs to stable parent-owned child profiles. At most five children are active across all families. Approved parent Google access and a manually reviewed invitation list remain; removing the public pilot-size sentence did not remove the cap.
- Owners add a Google email through one “Сподели достъп” section, without adult/child roles or a new invitation-acceptance step. The recipient signs in normally; bind an unclaimed grant only to the verified Google identity for that email. Keep the existing child and all saved work; never create a duplicate learner or globally approve another family.
- Everyone with access can learn, resume, view progress and rename the child (trimmed 1–40 characters). Only the original owner manages email access. Removing an email revokes its new and legacy connections and cancels old invitation links. Recheck authorization on protected requests; other children stay private.
- Keep existing child logins and accepted adult memberships working. Pending legacy invitations remain pending until accepted or explicitly replaced by an owner grant; migration alone grants no access. See [shared child access](docs/child-sharing.md) for security, copy and release evidence.
- Changing the selected child in another tab cannot retarget an open task. Drafts are scoped to child, competition, task and subcondition. Reject stale revisions rather than overwriting newer work; navigation waits for pending saves and retains the task on failure.
- Account/profile changes clear learner-scoped client caches. Reused competition snapshots do not replace server authorization or revision checks. Keep credentials and raw grading data out of browser responses.

## Progress and navigation

- Shared “Напредък” serves the child and authorized parents; `/parent` redirects to `/progress`. Read daily metrics from recorded history/rewards, grouped in Europe/Sofia with Monday first. Never invent dates or treat an imported snapshot as a fresh Supabase export.
- Count completed whole-task independent/hinted successes, including repeats, without duplicate gold. Revealed work is not successful solving and standalone subconditions are not completed tasks. New natural subparts count as completed steps, with separate subpart and whole-original completion metrics. Do not reinterpret historical subcondition events as whole originals or backfill dates. Show incorrect/revealed counts separately in day details; group the daily list by skill/competition instead of exposing a raw event ledger.
- Use one persistent next action during practice and preserve saved work across Home/Map/Pause. The full За сайта / Напредък / Профили navigation belongs on main views; quiet feedback remains separately available on hosted main, island, task and progress screens without unmounting the task. See the [design system](docs/design-system.md) for component, copy, accessibility and layout contracts.
- Two-day inactivity email/notification reminders remain future scope, separate from optional learning retention review. Channels, consent, schedule and repeat limits require an approved follow-up plan.
