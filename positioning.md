# Positioning plan

## Positioning
An interactive learning app for Bulgarian fourth-graders preparing for competitive maths. It helps children understand solutions, practise areas they find difficult and recognise their progress through short adventures.

Primary description: **Състезателна математика за 4. клас**

Supporting message: **Задачи с решения стъпка по стъпка, упражнения според напредъка и малки математически открития.**

## Audience and value
- Parents seeking useful independent practice alongside school or lessons.
- Children who need clear explanations, manageable challenges and encouragement to continue.
- Emphasise guided learning and choosing the next suitable exercise, rather than simply offering a problem library.
- Describe the app as independent practice; do not imply live tutoring or guaranteed competition results.

## Search visibility
- Main page: „Състезателна математика за 4. клас“.
- Publish useful, accessible topic pages with worked examples and links to practice.
- Start with the available „Математика за всеки“ material; advertise other collections only after review and publication.
- Measure relevant search impressions, visits and invitation requests through Search Console and privacy-conscious measurement.

## Name and domain
- Keep „Математически открития“ as the working app name until a final naming decision.
- Candidate domain: `sastezatelnamatematika.bg`, reported unused by the owner.
- Its strength is clarity; its weakness is length and limited distinctiveness.
- Domain selection remains open. Verify registration availability before purchase; do not expect the keywords alone to produce rankings.

## First validation
Show the landing page to the parents of the five pilot children. Check whether they understand the format, intended age and benefit, then improve the message before seeking more testers.
